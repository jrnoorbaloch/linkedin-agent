Features

Everything in LinkedIn Agent 2.0.0.

Write

Daily draft

Every posting day at your chosen time, a post is drafted from a pillar you haven't used lately, an unused story and today's headlines.

Post Writer with 20 hook formulas

Contrarian, time anchor, data reveal, R.I.P., anaphora and 15 more, chosen by your goal: comments, reposts, saves or leads.

Humanizer and audit

Scores a draft out of 100 and rewrites it without the phrases that make posts sound machine-written.

Carousels, polls, articles and video scripts

From one topic to a slide-by-slide carousel, a poll, a long-form article or a 60-second script.

Repurposer and evergreen recycler

Turn a blog, thread or transcript into a native post, or bring back your best posts with a new hook.

Approve and publish

Approval queue

Drafts, Approved, Scheduled and Published on one board. The API refuses to publish anything unapproved.

Live LinkedIn preview

See your post the way LinkedIn shows it, with the dashed line where “see more” cuts in.

Client review links

Send a signed link that works for 14 days. The client approves or asks for changes without an account.

Phone approvals

Telegram buttons or WhatsApp replies move a draft to approved.

Calendar

Drag to reschedule, and subscribe from Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

Media

Image generation

Uses your own OpenAI-compatible image model. A skill writes the prompt and alt text for you.

PDF carousel builder

Five themes, 1080×1350 slides, your name and page numbers, uploaded to LinkedIn as a document post.

Your own uploads

Attach any image or PDF from your computer.

Profile and career

Profile Optimizer

Score, three headline options, a seven-step About section, result-led experience bullets, skills, Featured and banner copy.

Headline A/B lab

Ten scored headline variants and a two-week test plan.

Resume, cover letter and job match

ATS-friendly resume with Word export, tailored letters and a gap analysis against any job description.

Interview prep and salary negotiation

Likely questions with STAR answers from your real experience, and a negotiation script.

Grow and learn

Import from LinkedIn

Your data export fills the brand profile, voice samples and post history. Your analytics export fills the metrics.

Analytics

Weekly impressions, a weekday-by-hour heatmap, and results by hook formula and pillar.

Trends

RSS headlines from your field, each turned into a post angle in one click.

Story bank and interviewer

The agent interviews you and keeps your real stories, so posts never invent facts.

Teams and agencies

Workspaces

One per LinkedIn account, each with its own brand, LinkedIn connection, queue and schedule.

Roles

Admins, editors who write, reviewers who approve and publish.

Company Pages and advocacy

Page posts, hiring posts, and one update turned into personal posts for every teammate.

Webhooks, Slack, CSV

Send every event to Zapier, Make or n8n, post to Slack, export to Sheets.

Everywhere

Desktop app

Windows installer, macOS app and Linux build, opening in a native window.

Any model

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Ollama or any compatible endpoint.

Claude skill, subagent and MCP server

Every skill available to Claude, Claude Code, Cursor and other agents.

Five languages

English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and Spanish, with right-to-left layouts.
