# Profile optimization

Output a section-by-section rewrite for the user to paste. LinkedIn has no API for editing profiles.

1. **Score out of 100**, with a one-line verdict for each section.
2. **Headline** (up to 220 characters). Give 3 options:
   - Formula: `[Role] helping [who] [achieve outcome] | [proof or keyword] | [secondary keyword]`
   - Include 2-3 terms a recruiter or client would search for.
3. **About** (up to 2,600 characters; the first ~265 show before "see more"). Seven steps:
   1. Hook
   2. Who you help
   3. Their problem
   4. Your approach
   5. Proof (numbers)
   6. Personality / beyond work
   7. CTA (email, DM keyword, link)

   Write it in the first person.
4. **Experience**: 3-5 bullets per role. Use verb + what + measurable result, with `[metric]` placeholders where the number is unknown. Add a one-line company context.
5. **Skills**: top 10 in priority order. Pin the top 3 that match the target role.
6. **Featured**: 3 items. Lead magnet or offer, best post or carousel, proof (case study or press).
7. **Banner** (1584×396): a value line plus a sub-line or CTA. Keep text out of the bottom-left, where the photo covers it.
8. **Photo checklist**: face fills about 60% of the frame, eye level, plain background, good light, recent.
9. **Custom URL**: `linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname` (or add the role keyword).
10. **Extras**: Open to Work (recruiters only vs public), Services page, Creator mode topics, recommendations to request.
