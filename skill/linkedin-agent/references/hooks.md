# Hook formulas (2026)

The hook is the first 1-2 lines (~210 characters) shown before "…see more". Its only job is to earn the click.

## Pick by engagement goal

| Goal | Best formulas |
|---|---|
| Comments | Contrarian, Question, False binary, Controlled A/B, Comment gate |
| Reposts | Numbered list, How I / How we, Myth vs reality, Data reveal |
| Saves | Numbered list, Before-after, Mistake/lesson, Behind the scenes |
| Profile visits / leads | Time anchor, Emotional cold open, Year pivot, How I |
| Followers | Anaphora, R.I.P., Named gratitude, Curiosity gap |

## The 20 formulas

1. **Contrarian**: "Unpopular opinion: [common advice] is costing you [outcome]."
2. **Curiosity gap**: "I changed one line in my [thing]. [Result] doubled."
3. **Time anchor**: "[N] years ago I [low point]. Today I [high point]."
4. **R.I.P.**: "R.I.P. [outdated practice]. Here's what replaced it."
5. **Anaphora**: repeat an opening phrase 3-4 times ("I used to… / I used to… / Now I…").
6. **Year pivot**: "In [year] I [belief]. In [year] I learned [opposite]."
7. **Numbered list**: "[N] [things] I wish I knew before [milestone]:"
8. **Emotional cold open**: start mid-scene. "The email said: 'We're letting you go.'"
9. **Mistake / lesson**: "I lost [cost] because I ignored [thing]."
10. **Before-after**: "Before: [pain]. After: [result]. The difference:"
11. **Question**: a sharp, specific question your audience argues about.
12. **Myth vs reality**: "Myth: [belief]. Reality: [truth]."
13. **Named gratitude**: "[Name] taught me [lesson] in one sentence."
14. **Controlled A/B**: "Option A: [x]. Option B: [y]. Which would you pick?"
15. **False binary**: "You can [easy path] or [hard path]. Only one works."
16. **Comment gate**: offer a resource for a keyword comment. Use sparingly; LinkedIn down-ranks engagement bait.
17. **Behind the scenes**: "Here's exactly how we [result], with numbers."
18. **Data reveal**: "We analyzed [N] [things]. [Surprising finding]."
19. **How I / How we**: "How I [result] in [timeframe] without [common method]."
20. **Short hot take**: one punchy sentence, then 3-5 lines of reasoning.

## Rules

- Be specific: numbers, names and timeframes beat adjectives.
- No "I'm excited to announce", "In today's fast-paced world", or a question with an obvious answer.
- The second line must pay off or deepen the first, so readers don't feel baited.

## Extract (reverse-engineer a viral post)

Return:
1. The formula used.
2. Three reasons it worked: specificity, tension, identity, novelty.
3. A structure outline.
4. A fill-in-the-blanks template.
5. One example adapted to the user's brand. Never copy the original's wording.
