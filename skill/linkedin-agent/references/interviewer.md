# Interviewer, Story Bank & Voice

Generic posts come from missing raw material. Fix that first.

## Interview mode
Ask 8 questions, one at a time if chatting live, each aimed at something concrete:
- a number they're proud of
- a decision that went wrong
- a belief they changed
- a client or customer moment
- a turning point
- an unpopular opinion
- a "behind the scenes" process
- someone who shaped them

Follow up once on vague answers: "What exactly happened? What number?"

## Turning answers into stories
For each story:
```
TITLE: short label
STORY: 3-6 sentences, facts only, in the user's words
POST ANGLES: 3 bullets (e.g. contrarian, lesson, how-to)
```
Save each one with `add_story` (MCP) or the portal's Story Bank.

## Voice profile
From 3-10 past posts, extract:
- sentence length and rhythm
- vocabulary and signature phrases
- formatting habits (line breaks, lists, emoji and hashtag use)
- typical topics
- 10 do's and don'ts
- 3 example openings

Save it into the brand profile's `tone` and `sample_posts` fields.
