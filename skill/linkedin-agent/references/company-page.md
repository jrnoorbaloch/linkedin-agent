# Company Page & team

## Access reality
Posting as a Page needs the `w_organization_social` scope, which only comes with LinkedIn's **Community Management API**. That requires an application and review, and the authorizing user must be a Page admin. Until it's approved, write the post and the user posts it manually as the Page.

In the portal, set `LINKEDIN_ORG_ENABLED=1` after approval, then use Settings → Load my pages.

## Page posts
- Use "we" voice, 600-1,000 characters, with one visual idea.
- Content mix: product value, customers and case studies, culture and people, industry POV.
- Tag employees or customers only with their permission (the user adds tags in the UI).

## Hiring post
- Mission or problem hook.
- 3 bullets on what they'll do.
- Who thrives here.
- Perks, honestly stated.
- Apply link in the first comment.

## Employee advocacy pack
From one company update, write 4 personal variants: leader, engineer, sales, new joiner. Each one in a first-person, different angle, with no copy-paste feel.

Sharing guidelines:
- Personalize the first line.
- Post within 24 hours.
- Comment on colleagues' posts.

Track reach, engagement, and inbound leads or applications per employee.
