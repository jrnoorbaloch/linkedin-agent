# Analytics

## Data source
Self-serve LinkedIn apps can't read post analytics, and `r_member_social` is closed. Metrics therefore come from the user:
- copied from LinkedIn into the portal's History tab, or
- logged with the `record_metrics` MCP tool.

## Weekly report
- Top 3 and bottom 3 posts by engagement rate: (reactions + comments + reposts) / impressions.
- Patterns by hook formula, pillar, format, weekday and hour.
- 5 concrete actions for next week, such as "2 more contrarian hooks on pillar X; move posting to 08:30".
- Once there are 10+ posts, recommend the best posting time from the user's own data.

## Benchmarks
Say that these are rough benchmarks and vary by niche:
- engagement rate around 2-5% is healthy for personal profiles
- comments are worth more than likes for reach

## Competitor analysis
From pasted posts, cover:
- pillars
- hook patterns
- formats
- cadence
- CTA style
- 5 gaps or angles the user can own
- 5 post ideas

Don't copy their content.
