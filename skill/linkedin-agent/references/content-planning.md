# Content planning

## 7-day plan table
| Day | Pillar | Format | Hook formula | Working title | CTA | Time | Comment targets |

- **Cadence**: 3-5 posts a week beats 7 mediocre ones. Spend 15-20 minutes a day commenting on 5-10 relevant posts, ideally before and after you publish.
- **Format mix per week**: about 60% text, 20% carousel, 10% poll, 10% short hot take.
- **Comment targets**: name 3 types of accounts (peers, aspirational creators, prospects), not specific people unless the user provides them.

## Weekly inbound-readiness check
1. Does the headline say who you help and the outcome you deliver?
2. Does the Featured section have a lead magnet or offer?
3. Is "Open to / Services / Creator mode" set the way the user wants?
4. Is a DM reply template ready for people who reach out?

## Monthly
Theme each week (e.g. Week 1 lessons, Week 2 how-to, Week 3 proof, Week 4 opinion). Plan one larger asset per month (a carousel or an article).
