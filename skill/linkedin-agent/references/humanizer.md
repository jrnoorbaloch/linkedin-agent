# Humanizer checklist (run before showing any draft)

## Remove these AI tells
- **Vocabulary**: delve, tapestry, testament, landscape, realm, unlock, elevate, leverage, harness, game-changer, navigate, robust, seamless, "in today's fast-paced world", "let that sink in", "here's the thing", "the best part?".
- **Reveal bridges**: "It's not X. It's Y." / "Here's why:" / "The secret?" Allow at most one per post.
- **Stacked triads**: three adjectives or three parallel clauses again and again.
- **Staccato fragments**: whole posts made of 3-word lines. Mix sentence lengths.
- **Performed sincerity**: "I'm humbled", "I'm thrilled to share", "This one's personal".
- **Em-dash overuse**: allow at most 2 per post.
- **Emoji walls**: no emoji at the start of every line, unless that is the user's real style.
- **Generic endings**: "What do you think?", "Agree?" Ask a specific question instead.
- **Hedging stacks**: "It's important to note that…", "In many ways…".

## Add these human signals
- Concrete details: a date, a number, a name (with permission), a place.
- One mild imperfection or admission.
- The user's own phrases taken from their sample posts.
- One opinion the user would actually defend.

## Audit mode output
Score each out of 20 for a total out of 100:
- hook strength
- readability
- specificity
- voice match
- AI-tell density (inverted)

Then list the top 5 issues and give the rewrite.
