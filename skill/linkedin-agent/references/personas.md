# Persona packs

Match the user to a pack, then follow its pillars, skills and weekly rhythm. In the app or over MCP, apply the pack with `apply_persona_pack`.

## 🎓 Students & Freshers (`student`)
Land internships and your first job by documenting what you learn and build.

- **Pillars:** Projects I built, What I'm learning, Internship/campus life, Career lessons
- **Tone:** Curious, humble, energetic
- **Goals:** Internships, first job, recruiter visibility
- **Key skills:** Project Showcase, Post Writer, Resume Builder, Connection Note, Skills Gap Planner, Interview Prep
- **Typical week:** Learning log → Project showcase → Lesson from a mistake → Resource list → Career question poll

## 💼 Job Seekers (`job_seeker`)
Get referrals and recruiter messages with a sharp profile and proof-of-work posts.

- **Pillars:** Wins & results, Industry opinions, Job search lessons, Skills in action
- **Tone:** Confident, specific, warm
- **Goals:** Interviews, referrals, recruiter inbound
- **Key skills:** Open-to-Work Post, Profile Optimizer, Headline A/B Lab, Job Match & Gap, Cover Letter, Recruiter / Candidate Outreach
- **Typical week:** Win story → Industry take → Open-to-work / ask → How I solved X → Thank-you / gratitude

## 💻 Developers & Engineers (`developer`)
Turn your code, repos and debugging stories into authority content.

- **Pillars:** Things I built, Debugging stories, Tools & stack, Engineering opinions, Career in tech
- **Tone:** Direct, practical, a little nerdy
- **Goals:** Job offers, freelance clients, open-source users
- **Key skills:** Project Showcase, Carousel Builder, Hook Extractor, Article / Newsletter, Trend Ideas, Resume Builder
- **Typical week:** Build log → Debugging story → Tool comparison carousel → Hot take → Repo showcase

## 🧑‍💻 Freelancers & Agencies (`freelancer`)
Attract clients with case studies, process posts and a profile that sells.

- **Pillars:** Client results, My process, Mistakes clients make, Behind the scenes, Offers
- **Tone:** Helpful, confident, results-first
- **Goals:** Inbound leads, client trust, referrals
- **Key skills:** Client Case Study Post, Social Selling Planner, Profile Optimizer, DM / InMail Writer, Carousel Builder, 7-Day Content Planner
- **Typical week:** Case study → Process carousel → Common mistake → Behind the scenes → Soft offer

## 📈 Sales & Business Development (`sales`)
Warm up prospects with social selling and raise your SSI.

- **Pillars:** Customer problems, Industry insights, Wins & lessons, Buyer education
- **Tone:** Consultative, curious, no hard pitch
- **Goals:** Meetings booked, pipeline, SSI
- **Key skills:** Social Selling Planner, Comment Drafter, Connection Note, DM / InMail Writer, Client Case Study Post, Weekly Performance Report
- **Typical week:** Buyer problem → Insight/data → Customer story → Myth vs reality → Question poll

## 🚀 Founders & Creators (`founder`)
Build in public, grow an audience and turn it into customers and hires.

- **Pillars:** Building in public, Lessons & failures, Industry POV, Team & hiring, Product
- **Tone:** Honest, opinionated, story-driven
- **Goals:** Followers, customers, hiring, investors
- **Key skills:** Post Writer, 7-Day Content Planner, Hiring Post, Employee Advocacy Pack, Evergreen Recycler, Competitor Analysis
- **Typical week:** Build-in-public update → Failure lesson → Contrarian take → Team story → Product insight

## 🩺 Doctors, Teachers & Experts (`professional`)
Share expertise simply and responsibly to build trust in your field.

- **Pillars:** Myths vs facts, Practical tips, Stories from practice, Field news explained
- **Tone:** Clear, kind, trustworthy
- **Goals:** Reputation, patients/students/clients, speaking
- **Key skills:** Expert Explainer, Post Writer, Carousel Builder, Video Script, Profile Optimizer, Trend Ideas
- **Typical week:** Myth vs fact → Practical tips carousel → Story from practice → News explained → Q&A poll

## 🧲 Recruiters & HR (`recruiter`)
Employer branding, hiring posts and candidate outreach that gets replies.

- **Pillars:** Open roles, Culture stories, Hiring tips for candidates, Market insights
- **Tone:** Warm, transparent, helpful
- **Goals:** Qualified applicants, employer brand
- **Key skills:** Hiring Post, Recruiter / Candidate Outreach, Employee Advocacy Pack, Company Page Post, Post Writer, Interview Prep
- **Typical week:** Open role → Culture story → Candidate tip → Market insight → Team spotlight
