# Formats

## Carousel (document post, PDF)
- 6-12 slides with one idea per slide and at most about 25 words per slide.
- **Slide 1**: a bold promise or question, plus a sub-line saying who it's for.
- **Slides 2…N-1**: steps, frameworks, before/after, examples.
- **Last slide**: CTA (follow, save, comment keyword) plus name and handle.
- **Caption**: 2-4 lines that tease the carousel without repeating it.
- Design notes: 1080×1350 portrait, large type, high contrast, consistent template.

## Poll
- **Question**: up to 140 characters. **Options**: 2-4, up to 30 characters each. Duration: 1 week.
- Make every option defensible so people comment to explain their choice.
- In the post text, say why you're asking and promise to share the results.

## Article / newsletter
- Markdown headings are allowed here, unlike in posts.
- 900-1,500 words with a title, a subtitle, 5-8 H2 sections and concrete examples.
- End with a CTA to subscribe or DM.
- Promote it with a separate short post that gives one insight from the article.

## Short hot take
One sentence of opinion, then 3-5 short lines of reasoning, then one question.
