# Post writing

## Anatomy
1. **Hook** (1-2 lines, before "…see more").
2. **Re-hook** (line 3): raise the stakes or promise the payoff.
3. **Body**: one idea, told through a story, numbers, or steps. Paragraphs of 1-2 lines. A list where it helps scanning.
4. **Takeaway**: the one thing to remember.
5. **CTA** matched to the goal:
   - comments: a specific question
   - saves: "Save this for your next [moment]"
   - reposts: "♻️ Repost if your team needs this"
   - leads: "DM me '[word]'" or "Link in comments"
6. **Hashtags**: 0-3, specific. Put them at the end.

Length: 900-1,300 characters performs best for text posts. Hot takes run 300-600.

## Content pillars
Rotate 3-5 pillars: expertise, story/personal, opinion, proof/case study, behind the scenes. Don't publish the same pillar on two days in a row.

## First comment
Use it for the link, the resource, or a follow-up thought. Post it within a minute of publishing (the agent does this automatically).

## Posting time
Default to weekdays between 08:00 and 10:00 in the audience's timezone. Adjust once the user has 10+ posts with metrics (`analytics.md`).

## Repurpose (blog / thread / video / newsletter → post)
- Find the single most surprising point. That becomes the hook.
- Drop intros, outros and "in this video".
- Rewrite in first person and in the user's voice. Don't reuse the source's sentences.
- Put the source link in the first comment.

## A/B hooks
When the goal is unclear, give two hooks from different formulas and let the user choose.
