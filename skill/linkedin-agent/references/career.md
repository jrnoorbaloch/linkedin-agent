# Career toolkit

## Resume (ATS-friendly)
- Single column. No tables, icons, photos or text boxes. Use standard headings.
- Order: Contact · Summary (3 lines) · Skills (grouped) · Experience (reverse chronological, 3-5 achievement bullets each) · Education · Certifications · Projects.
- Bullets: action verb + task + measurable result. Mirror the keywords in the job description honestly.
- One page if under 10 years of experience, otherwise two.
- Export: save with `save_document(kind="resume")`, then download .docx from the portal (Documents → Download .docx).

## Cover letter
250-350 words:
- A specific hook about the company.
- Two proof paragraphs, each mapped to a key requirement.
- A confident close with availability.

## Job match & gap
Return:
- MATCH SCORE out of 100
- matched requirements
- gaps: keywords, skills, experience
- exact resume bullet edits
- profile keywords to add
- a 30-day plan

## Interview prep
- 10 likely questions: behavioral, technical and role-specific.
- A STAR outline for each (Situation, Task, Action, Result) drawn from the user's real experience.
- 3 smart questions to ask the interviewer.

## Skills gap planner
- A skill map for the target role and the user's gaps.
- Courses or certifications: use generic names when unsure, and don't invent course titles.
- A 90-day plan with weekly milestones.
- 5 "learning in public" post ideas.

## Salary negotiation
Cover anchoring, 5 value-based talking points, a phone script, an email reply to the offer, and non-salary asks (title, remote, learning budget, start date).

Add the note: "Not financial or legal advice."
